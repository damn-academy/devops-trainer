<!-- section: hook -->
A salesperson decided that a telecom operator's head of infrastructure should see his company everywhere. He followed all the manager's social media accounts, liked every post, found his personal number, messaged the manager's wife on Facebook to “say hello,” and arrived at the operator's office without an invitation. A week later, the operator's security team added the salesperson's company to its list of unwanted vendors. Over six months, another salesperson spoke at two meetups attended by the operator's engineers, fixed a bug in an open-source project the operator used, and invited the manager to a dinner for eight telecom engineers discussing platform upgrades. The manager asked for a meeting himself. This lesson explains how to be where the right person spends time and earn their trust without crossing the line between persistence and harassment.

<!-- section: theory | Advertising to a list of companies -->
LinkedIn lets you show ads to people at selected companies and to an uploaded contact list, but a campaign can run only if LinkedIn has matched at least three hundred members in the audience. That means three hundred people; three hundred companies or file rows do not guarantee the threshold. Country and job-title filters reduce the audience, and a small Dream 100 program may fall below the threshold once you keep only the relevant people. Do not add unrelated people just to reach it. In the European Economic Area and Switzerland, matching depends on members' consent.

Group companies by a shared problem, such as tenant separation at hosting providers, moving away from virtualization at large companies, or platform upgrades. Exclude current customers, check audience expansion settings, and keep a small group without ads to compare whether conversations increase. Reddit advertising works differently: it targets people who have visited selected communities in the past 28 days and does not know which of them work at your bank.

<!-- section: theory | A useful presence -->
The best way to be present is to do something useful where these people spend time. Write about a real constraint faced by companies in your segment and send the relevant person the section that addresses their problem. For a hosting provider, this means tenant isolation, per-tenant operations, and economics per unit of hardware; for a large company, upgrade and rollback tests, support responsibilities, and migration risks. Show versions, hardware, commands, and failure cases. Do not put a company's logo on a page that looks like a customer story, and do not present a guess about its architecture as fact.

Ask contacts at the companies you want to reach what they read, listen to, and attend, and record those places in the account map. Offer a technical talk or article there, disclosing your employer. In 2025, Victoria Melnikova of Evil Martians interviewed the founders of Sentry, WorkOS, Resend, PlanetScale, Chroma, and Mastra in person; this practitioner conversation format opens doors, although nobody has measured the sales it generates. Fix a reproducible bug, add a missing test, or improve documentation in an open-source project the company uses, following that project's rules. An accepted pull request demonstrates your work, but does not oblige anyone to buy or authorize you to collect maintainers' contact details; do not add advertising to review discussions.

<!-- section: theory | The limits of persistence -->
An acceptable pattern is to answer a public technical question with a reproducible solution, ask once whether a private conversation would help, and accept the answer. An unacceptable pattern is for a salesperson to call someone's personal number after a refusal, message their spouse and manager, repeatedly tag them in posts, or write from another account. Silence does not authorize adding channels indefinitely. Do not use home addresses or family members, pretend to have a relationship that never existed, or include personal details from someone's life in emails. Maintain a shared contact suppression table and check each person's contact frequency.

<!-- section: theory | How to earn trust -->
Engineers and managers trust people who provide verifiable value. Offer a free review of the company's published architecture based on a public talk, diagram, or repository. Keep it to one page, distinguish observations, assumptions, and remaining questions, and include two tradeoffs and one reproducible experiment. Do not scan their infrastructure or imply a vulnerability without permission. “Let's compare our tenant isolation test plan with your published requirements” works; “everything you have is broken” closes the door.

A benchmark is credible when it includes exact versions, hardware, storage, network, dataset, cache state, number of repetitions, variability, load generator, and raw results, with engineering conclusions separated from cost estimates. Include a result unfavorable to you and the conditions under which the customer's current solution is better. The published Michelin case study, in which rebuilding Kubernetes with open-source tools reduced platform costs by 44 % and upgrade preparation time by 85 %, while the platform grew to 62 clusters across 42 locations, is useful as an example of the metrics buyers measure. It does not promise the same results for you.

Introduce an operator who has solved the same problem to someone currently solving it only with both parties' consent. Offer an incident review template, a migration decision tree, or a conversation with a maintainer without requiring a meeting in return. PostHog's sales principles emphasize technical competence, transparent answers, and turning down deals that are not a fit.

<!-- section: theory | Roundtables, user advisory boards, and references -->
An invitation to a user advisory board should state the topic, preparation time, whether compensation is offered, confidentiality rules, and which product decisions the participant can influence. Do not call a sales demo a user advisory board. For a company's first salesperson, a small operator roundtable on upgrades or tenant isolation is more honest than an honorary title with no authority.

Prepare a buyer's conversation with an existing customer, known as a reference, as follows: first identify the buyer's technical uncertainty, select a customer with similar scale and constraints, ask permission for this specific conversation, send three questions, and let them speak candidly. For infrastructure, useful questions cover incidents, upgrades, operations staffing, migration surprises, and support escalations; “do you like the product?” tells you little.

<!-- section: task | Assignment: a presence plan for one company -->
Complete the assignment in your Dream 100 template from the first lesson of the module. For one tier A company, record three places its engineers spend time and how you learned this. Choose an open-source project the company uses and find a contribution you can make: an issue to fix if you are an engineer, or an answer to a user question, a documentation correction or a translation if you are not. You can also ask an engineer at your company to take the issue while you prepare its description. Prepare a one-page review of the company’s published architecture, marking “visible”, “assumed” and “questions”. If you are not an engineer, assemble it from the company’s talks and articles and ask an engineer for a fifteen-minute review. Then reread your plan through that company’s security team’s eyes and remove anything that would look like stalking.
